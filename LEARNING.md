# 🧠 Megaspy's Learning Roadmap

Start easy and slow. The **game comes first**: she picks a game, and the logic grows out of building it.
One small idea per session. Always end in success.

## Level 1: IF … THEN
- One rule inside the game she picked. *"IF you catch the snitch THEN 150 points!"*
- She sees it happen on Sparkle / the yellow screen, then sees the one real line of code:
  `if (caught) { points = points + 150; }`
- Warm-up with no computer: "Robot Rules". The grown-up is the robot; she gives the rules ("IF I clap THEN you jump").

## Level 2: ELSE
- What happens otherwise? *"IF you have the key THEN treasure ELSE dragon!"*
- Showing it: `pickers/07-if-then-else.html` (parked: train tracks / portrait / potion pipes / Quidditch / Gringotts /
  owl post, each a token that travels to a fork and goes to THEN or ELSE). Use her pick there when we reach this level.

## Level 3: Try again (gentle loops)
- *"IF it didn't work, try again."* The game makes sure she **wins within a couple of tries**, so she learns that
  trying again pays off, not that she failed.

## Level 4: Friends and feelings (social IF / ELSE)
The grown-up's idea: logic for playing with friends, always leading to a happy ending.
- *"Ask a friend to play Quidditch."* → **IF** yes **THEN** play together!
- **ELSE** (they don't want to): what else could we do? → suggest another game, ask what *they* would like,
  take turns choosing, or play side by side.
- Sparkle (or the yellow screen) plays the friend, with a feelings face (😊 😐 😢). It says "no" once or twice, then
  "yes!" to one of her ideas. Every path ends with them playing together.
- Talk about it: *How does Sparkle feel? What could you try next?*

## Level 5: AND / OR / NOT
- *"IF clap AND tilted THEN Lumos"*, *"IF NOT quiet THEN shh!"*

## Level 6: 🐞 Find the Bug
- The AI writes a rule that's wrong on purpose (*"IF you catch the snitch THEN lose points"*?!) and she spies the
  mistake and fixes it. Spy + hacking + logic.

Badges for each level go in `IDEAS.md`.
